FAQ

Straight answers.

Is PetSignal free? +

Yes. Paw checks, off-leash maps, hazard warnings and lost & found are free and always will be. The optional Pack subscription adds multi-dog profiles, a 7-day safety outlook and full walk history.

How accurate is the paw check? +

It's a model calibrated against published veterinary data — at 25°C air temperature, full-sun asphalt can reach roughly 52°C, hot enough to burn a paw in about a minute. Treat it as strong guidance, and always confirm with the back-of-hand test before you walk.

Do you track me? +

No. Location is only used while the app is open, to check conditions where you are and find nearby off-leash areas. There's no background tracking, no advertising SDKs, and we never sell or share your data.

When's Android coming? +

iOS first — no date yet for Android. We're building for iPhone owners now and will share news when there's something real to say.

How do I join the beta? +

Tap "Join the TestFlight beta" above, install Apple's TestFlight app if you don't have it, then follow the invite link to install PetSignal.
