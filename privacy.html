🐾 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026 · Applies to the PetSignal iOS app

PetSignal is built local-first: the most private place for your data is your own phone, so that is where it lives and where the app reads and writes it. The app works fully offline and no account is required to use it.

There is one exception, and you choose it: if you sign in, your dogs and walks are backed up to your own account so that a new phone, or reinstalling the app, doesn’t lose them. Nothing is uploaded while you are signed out.

What is backed up to your account (only if you sign in)

  • Walk routes and history — including the route itself, which is precise location data.
  • Dog profiles and photos — as you entered them.

These are stored on our Australian server (Sydney) and are readable only by your own account. Database-level rules restrict every row to the account that created it; no other user, signed in or not, can read them. We do not share, sell, aggregate or publish them, and they are never shown to another user. Signing out leaves the copy on your phone untouched. Deleting your account deletes the backup with it.

What stays on your device (never uploaded)

  • AI conversations — Apple’s on-device model and the bundled guide stay on your phone. When the clearly labelled PetSignal AI fallback is used, your question plus the dog’s name, size, vibe and any health context you chose to record are sent to our Supabase function and its model provider to produce the answer. We do not send your identity, location, routes, photos or videos, and we do not retain the conversation as app content.
  • Saved and smart places — the places your walks tend to end, kept only on your phone.
  • Park community drafts, posts and media — while the public community service is disabled, anything you create in The Park remains on your phone and is not uploaded. We will update this policy and ask for agreement before public sharing is enabled.

What is shared with the community (only when you post it)

  • Hazard reports (snake, tick, glass, plant sightings…): the pin location, category and note you write.
  • Lost & found reports: the details you enter, including a contact number if you choose to add one — its whole purpose is to be seen by nearby owners.
  • Dog-friendly places you add, with their amenity tags.

These are stored on our Australian server (Sydney) with no account attached — we do not link reports to an identity. Reports expire automatically (hazards up to 30 days by type; lost & found after 14 days).

Location

Location is used to show conditions where you are, find nearby off-leash areas, and record your walk. We ask only for “When In Use” permission and never for always-on background access; while a walk is actually recording, location continues in the background so the route isn’t broken when your screen locks, which iOS shows you with its status indicator. No other user is ever shown your precise location. If you are signed in, the recorded route is backed up to your own account as described above.

Weather

Conditions use Apple WeatherKit first. If Apple Weather is temporarily unavailable, PetSignal falls back to Open-Meteo. Weather requests contain approximate coordinates and no account identity. Open-Meteo data is provided under CC BY 4.0. PetSignal clearly labels its separate pavement-temperature estimate.

What we don’t do

  • No account is required. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive Apple’s private account identifier and the email/name you choose to share, solely for account functionality.
  • No tracking and no advertising SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No analytics that identify you.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your device — and, because that copy is the only one when you are signed out, it cannot be recovered. Optional accounts can be permanently deleted inside PetSignal under Dogs → Account; deleting the account also deletes the dogs and walks backed up to it. See data deletion.

Children

PetSignal is not directed at children under 16.
